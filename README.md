<p align="center"><strong>hon</strong> is a calm reading planner that turns your book list into a schedule you can keep. <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phosphor-icons/core/main/assets/regular/book-open-text.svg" width="16" height="16" alt="" align="absmiddle" /></p>

- <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phosphor-icons/core/main/assets/regular/magnifying-glass.svg" width="16" height="16" alt="" align="absmiddle" /> Find books by title or add them yourself.
- <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phosphor-icons/core/main/assets/regular/book-open.svg" width="16" height="16" alt="" align="absmiddle" /> Track your progress as you read and edit a book's page count.
- <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phosphor-icons/core/main/assets/regular/arrows-down-up.svg" width="16" height="16" alt="" align="absmiddle" /> Reorder books in the schedule, which opens as compact colored spines. Expand cards to see their covers.
- <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phosphor-icons/core/main/assets/regular/calendar-check.svg" width="16" height="16" alt="" align="absmiddle" /> Choose your reading days and either set a daily page goal or a finish date.
- <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/phosphor-icons/core/main/assets/regular/books.svg" width="16" height="16" alt="" align="absmiddle" /> Read books one at a time or share your daily pages across several books.

Your plan updates as your list, pace and progress change.
